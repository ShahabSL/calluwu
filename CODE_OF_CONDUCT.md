# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Calluwu contributors, maintainers, and community members commit to making
participation respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of
background, identity, experience, appearance, disability, or viewpoint.

We value technical rigor, curiosity, direct but humane feedback, and decisions
grounded in evidence. Disagreement is welcome; intimidation, personal attacks,
sexualized attention, doxxing, threats, and sustained disruption are not.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the work and its impact, not a person's worth or motives.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good faith until evidence says otherwise.
- Protect security reports, credentials, personal data, and private
  communications.
- Respect maintainer decisions and use the documented appeal path.
- Correct mistakes openly and make reasonable repair when harm occurs.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, reviews, release spaces,
and public events where someone represents Calluwu. Maintainers may edit or
remove abusive content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or
permanently ban a participant. Enforcement will be proportional to impact and
pattern, while protecting reporters from retaliation.

Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's **Security → Report a
vulnerability** flow and mark the report as a conduct concern. Include only the
minimum evidence needed; do not publish private or identifying information.
Maintainers will acknowledge the report, avoid conflicts of interest, and share
the outcome with affected parties when safe.

This policy is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant
2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
